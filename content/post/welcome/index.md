than skim ten. So instead of a static résumé, I keep a working notebook: what I'm
building, what broke, and what I learned fixing it.

If you're a recruiter or engineer in Berlin looking at my
[projects](/projects/), this blog is the "why" behind them. If you're learning
MLOps yourself, maybe some of it saves you a wrong turn.
