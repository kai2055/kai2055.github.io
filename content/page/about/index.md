## What I care about

Most ML portfolios show a model that runs once. I care about the part most
tutorials skip: keeping systems **working after they ship**. Data validated
before it enters, models watched in production, and failures turned into
measurable, deployment-gating feedback.

One principle, three layers — data, model, system. My [projects](/projects/)
page walks through each one.
