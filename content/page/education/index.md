## Bachelor's in Information Management (BIM)

**Tribhuvan University** — Thames International College, Kathmandu · 2019–2024 ·
**CGPA 3.66 / 4.0**

A four-year, eight-semester degree under the Faculty of Management, combining a
full computing curriculum with management foundations. The technical coursework
is where my software base comes from:

- **Programming & CS foundations:** Structured Programming, Java Programming I &
  II, Object-Oriented Analysis & Design, Data Structure & Algorithm with Java,
  Computer Organization, Digital Logic Design
- **Data & databases:** Database Management System, Data Mining & Data
  Warehousing, Business Statistics
- **AI & systems:** Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Software
  Project Management, Management Information System
- **Web & networking:** Web Programming I & II, Data Communication & Computer
  Networks, Advanced Internetworking, Computer Graphics
- **Mathematics:** Basic Mathematics, Discrete Mathematics & Its Applications

The degree also included a summer project, an internship, and IT
entrepreneurship coursework — the mix of building and shipping that led me
toward engineering.
